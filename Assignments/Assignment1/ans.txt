Problem (unicode2)
(a): UTF-8 完全兼容 Unicode，根据不同的 Unicode code point 灵活选择 1/2/3/4 bytes 来表示，UTF-16 对大部分字符都用 2 bytes，UTF-32 都使用 4 bytes；因此对于相同的给定文本， UTF-8 通常情况下的编码字节数更短
(b): 这里说明 UTF-8 的编码规则：

Unicode 范围              UTF-8 二进制格式

U+0000   ~ U+007F         0xxxxxxx

U+0080   ~ U+07FF         110xxxxx 10xxxxxx

U+0800   ~ U+FFFF         1110xxxx 10xxxxxx 10xxxxxx

U+10000  ~ U+10FFFF       11110xxx 10xxxxxx 10xxxxxx 10xxxxxx

因此不能对单个字节直接调用 decode()，否则有可能会报错，例如以 10 开头的字节没有起始字节。
报错示例：UnicodeDecodeError: 'utf-8' codec can't decode byte 0xe4 in position 0: unexpected end of data
(c): 逻辑同 b，可以用 11111111 11111111，第二个字节不以 10 开头

Increasing the context length from 1,024 to 16,384 (16×) increases the total FLOPs for one forward pass from approximately 3.68 TFLOPs to 136.21 TFLOPs, representing a 37.00× increase.

The proportion of FLOPs consumed by attention increases significantly from 8.75% to 60.54%, while the proportion consumed by SwiGLU decreases from 54.96% to 23.77%. This is because attention has quadratic complexity \(O(L^2)\) with respect to sequence length, whereas the other components scale linearly with \(L\). Consequently, attention becomes the dominant computational bottleneck for long-context inference.

Problem(adamw_accounting)
(a) peak memory:
P = 2VD + L(4D^2 + 2D + 3DF) + 2VD
M_params = 4P
M_activations = 4B[L(8TD + 4TF + 2HT^2) + 2TD + 2TV]
M_grad = 4P
M_optimizer = 8P 

TOTAL: 16P + 4B[L(8TD + 4TF + 2HT^2) + 2TD + 2TV]

how to compute activation?
    embedding: (B, T, D) BTD
    transformer block: 
        2 * RMSNorm: (B, T, D) 2BTD
        multihead_self_attention:
            qkv projection: (B, T, D) 3BTD
            attention scores: (B, H, T, T) BHT^2
            softmax: BHT^2
            weight sum: (B, T, D) BTD
            output projection: (B, T, D) BTD

            total: 5BTD + 2BHT^2
        swiglu ffn:
            XW1: (B, T, F) BTF
            XW2: (B, T, F) BTF
            SiLU: (B, T, F) BTF
            element-wise multiplication: (B, T, F) BTF
            W3 projection: (B, T, D) BTD

            total: 4BTF + BTD
        
        total: 8BTD + 2BHT^2 + 4BTF
    final rmsnorm: (B, T, D) BTD
    final linear: (B, T, V) BTV
    cross entropy: (B, T, V) BTV
